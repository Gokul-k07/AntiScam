What ScamShield Can Flag

Lookalike Typosquats & Phishing Stacks: Domains engineered to visually abuse schedule commercial banks, parcel logistics (India Post, BlueDart), and utility portals via unauthorized top-level suffixes.
Coercive Social Engineering Constructs: Known extortion algorithms, electricity immediate blackout threats, fake judicial notices, and unauthorized police summons invoking rapid asset freeze threats.
Non-Compliant SMS Senders: Payloads originating from 10-digit mobile numbers (+91...) falsely claiming to be transactional alerts, directly violating TRAI DLT commercial header standards.
Inbound UPI Collect Reversal Tricks: VPA collection requests falsely disguised with descriptions like “Approved Refund”, “Cashback Credit”, or “Army Official Verification”.

What ScamShield Can't Tell You

Absence of Trigger Is Not an Endorsement: Zero matched heuristic flags does not guarantee absolute safety. Novel domains created within the last hour may not match known heuristic patterns immediately.
Zero Backend Telemetry & Storage: The system does not send your query to an external intelligence server or save text strings. Checks execute isolated in memory inside your web browser.
Irreversible UPI Settlement: Once a 4 or 6 digit UPI PIN is submitted on NPCI infrastructure, the software cannot halt or reverse the clearing transaction. Only immediate reporting via 1930 can trigger freeze protocols.
Not a Law Enforcement Action: This application is an educational pre-flight diagnostic tool. It does not replace a formal First Information Report (FIR) filed through official state police mechanisms.
